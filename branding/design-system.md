# 🎨 SkinSkill Design System

## 🌌 Vision
The SkinSkill visual identity is built on the concept of **Autonomous Cognition and Modular Execution**. It represents a sophisticated, invisible layer of intelligence that wraps around the developer's environment.

## 💎 Core Symbol
- **The Hexagon:** Represents modularity, structure, and the "Skill" units.
- **The Pulsing Core:** Represents the "Neural Brain" and persistent memory.
- **The Connection Nodes:** Represents the MCP bridges to external IDEs and environments.

## 🎨 Color Palette
- **Deep Carbon (#0A0A0B):** The foundation. Dark, professional, cinematic.
- **Neural Purple (#6366F1):** Intelligence, cognition, and the AI layer.
- **Success Mint (#10B981):** Execution, healing, and stable builds.
- **Ghost White (#F8FAFC):** Clarity and precision typography.

## 🔡 Typography
- **Primary:** Inter or San Francisco (Clean, geometric, high readability).
- **Secondary (Monospace):** JetBrains Mono (For engineering excellence).

## 📐 Usage Guidelines
- Use the **Icon** for UI buttons and favicons.
- Use the **Full Logo** for headers and documentation starts.
- Use the **Banner** for social previews and top-of-README impact.
